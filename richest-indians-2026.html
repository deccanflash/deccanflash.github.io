India's 10 richest people in 2026

$112.7BGautam Adani & family, India's richest · Forbes India 100 Richest 2026

Gautam Adani is back at No. 1 on the Forbes list of India's 100 richest, worth $112.7 billion, ahead of Mukesh Ambani at $86.3 billion. Together, India's 100 richest people are worth about $1 trillion, the same as last year. The top 10 alone hold $432.9 billion.

Quick answers

Who is the richest person in India in 2026?

Gautam Adani and family, worth $112.7 billion on the Forbes India 100 Richest 2026 list. Mukesh Ambani is No. 2 with $86.3 billion.

Who is the richest woman in India?

Savitri Jindal, No. 3 on the 2026 list with $39.8 billion.

How much do India's 100 richest own together?

About $1 trillion, unchanged from last year. The minimum net worth to make the list fell to $3 billion from $3.2 billion, and there are four newcomers.
