India's GDP

$3.92 trillionNominal GDP, 2025 (IMF, Apr 2026) · world rank #6

India's economy is about 12 times bigger in dollar terms than in 1990. In 2025 it ranks 6th, behind the USA, China, Germany, Japan and the UK. That is a statistical slip, not a slowdown: the rupee fell from about ₹84.6 to ₹88.5 per dollar, and India's new GDP series (base year 2022-23) is smaller than the old one.

Quick answers

What is India's GDP in 2025?

About $3.92 trillion in nominal US dollar terms, according to the IMF's April 2026 World Economic Outlook.

Is India the 4th largest economy?

Not in 2025. Earlier IMF forecasts put India 4th, but the April 2026 figures rank it 6th, behind Japan ($4.44T) and the UK ($4.00T), because of the weaker rupee and India's GDP base revision. India is still the fastest-growing major economy. See the full world economies 2025 dashboard.
