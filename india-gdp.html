Quick answers

What is India's GDP in 2025?

About $4.19 trillion in nominal US dollar terms, according to the IMF's April 2025 World Economic Outlook.

Is India the 4th largest economy?

Yes, by the IMF's 2025 estimate India ($4.187T) is just ahead of Japan ($4.186T). The gap is tiny, so rankings can shift with exchange rates.
