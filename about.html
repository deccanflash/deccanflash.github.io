About Deccan Flash

Deccan Flash turns official data about India and the world into short charts and plain-English explainers.

We started on Instagram (@deccanflash) with data reels on India's economy, gold and world rankings. This site keeps the numbers in one place, with sources, so you can check them yourself.

How we work

  • Numbers come from official or primary sources: RBI, NPCI, World Bank, IMF, FAO, UN, worldsteel, OICA and similar.
  • Every chart and table names its source and the period it covers.
  • When a source updates or we find a mistake, we correct the page and change the "Updated" date.
  • We explain data. We do not give stock tips or investment advice.
