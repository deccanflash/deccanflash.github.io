What drove 2025

  • Metals ran on fear and rate cuts. Central-bank buying, geopolitical tension and expected US rate cuts pushed gold past $4,480 an ounce for the first time in December 2025.
  • PSU banks and metals led stocks. State-run banks beat the Nifty for the fifth year in a row.
  • IT, media and realty lagged. All three ended 2025 lower, while the Nifty 50 rose for a 10th straight year.
  • A weaker rupee. The rupee lost about 5% against the dollar in 2025, which also lifted rupee prices of gold and silver.

Quick answers

Which was the best investment in India in 2025?

Silver, up about 122% in 2025, followed by gold at about 72%. The Nifty 50 rose 10.5%.

Which Nifty sector did best in 2025?

Nifty PSU Bank, up 27.7%, followed by Metal (+24.0%) and Auto (+21.9%).

Which sector did worst?

Nifty Media fell 22.2%, Realty fell 16.1% and IT fell 9.7%.

Did small caps make money in 2025?

No. The Nifty Smallcap 250 fell about 7.2%, even as large caps gained.
