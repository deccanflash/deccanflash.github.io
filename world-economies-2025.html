The world's 20 biggest economies in 2025

The US economy ($30.8 trillion) is about 1.6 times China's, and bigger than the next nine economies after China combined (Germany to Brazil: $30.5 trillion). India ranks 6th at $3.92 trillion, just $87 billion behind the UK.

Why India is 6th, not 4th

Earlier IMF forecasts had India passing Japan in 2025. The April 2026 numbers changed that for two statistical reasons. First, the rupee weakened from about ₹84.6 to ₹88.5 per dollar, and the IMF ranks in dollars. Second, India moved its GDP base year from 2011-12 to 2022-23 in February 2026, and the new series is smaller (FY26 nominal GDP fell from ₹357 lakh crore to ₹345.5 lakh crore). In rupee terms India still grew about 9%, and it remains the fastest-growing major economy.

Quick answers

What is the largest economy in the world in 2025?

The United States, at about $30.77 trillion in nominal GDP (IMF, April 2026).

Where does India rank in 2025?

6th, at about $3.92 trillion, behind the USA, China, Germany, Japan and the UK.

How much bigger is the US economy than China's?

About $11.1 trillion bigger, or roughly 1.57 times China's size in dollar terms.
