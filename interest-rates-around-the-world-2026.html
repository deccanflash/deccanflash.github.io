Quick answers

What is India's interest rate now?

The RBI repo rate is 5.50%, after a 0.25 percentage point hike on 7 October 2026. The next RBI policy meeting is on 4 December 2026.

Which major country has the highest interest rate?

Turkey, at 36% (one-week repo rate), after a 1 percentage point cut on 22 October 2026, its first cut in 9 months.

What is the US interest rate?

The Federal Reserve's target range is 3.75–4.00%, after a 0.25 point hike on 16 September 2026, its first hike since 2023. The Fed meets next on 27–28 October.

Which country has a 0% interest rate?

Switzerland. The Swiss National Bank kept its policy rate at 0% in September 2026.
