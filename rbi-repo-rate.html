Interest rates

RBI repo rate

5.25%Unchanged at the August 2026 meeting · next decision 7 Oct 2026, 10 AM

The repo rate is the rate at which banks borrow short-term money from the Reserve Bank of India. When it goes down, loans usually get cheaper. When it goes up, EMIs usually rise.

What it means for your EMI

Example: a ₹50 lakh home loan over 20 years, assuming your loan rate is the repo rate plus 2.75% and your bank passes on the full change.

Illustrative only. Your actual rate depends on your bank, loan type and credit profile.

Who decides

  • The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC): 6 members, 3 from the RBI and 3 appointed by the government.
  • It meets about every two months.
  • Its target is 4% inflation, within a 2–6% band. Retail inflation was 4.82% in August 2026.

Quick answers

What is the current repo rate?

5.25%, held unchanged in August 2026. The next decision is announced on 7 October 2026 at 10 AM.

What was the lowest repo rate?

4%, set in May 2020 during COVID.

Does a repo rate cut lower my EMI immediately?

Only for loans linked to an external benchmark like the repo rate, and usually from the next reset date. Ask your bank whether your loan is RLLR-linked.
