Why gold prices rise in India

  • A weaker rupee. Gold is priced in US dollars worldwide, so when the rupee falls, gold costs more in India.
  • Crises and wars. People and governments buy gold as a safe place for money.
  • Central banks buying. Central banks bought over 1,000 tonnes a year in 2022–24.
  • Duties and festivals. Import duty and wedding and festival demand, especially around Dhanteras.

Dhanteras in 2026 falls on Friday, 6 November.

Quick answers

What is the gold rate today in India?

24K gold was ₹1,47,785 per 10 grams on 5 October 2026 (22K: ₹1,35,371). Rates differ slightly between cities and jewellers add making charges.

What was the gold price in 1947?

About ₹88 per 10 grams, as widely reported.

Has gold always gone up?

No. Gold fell in rupee terms in several years, for example 1976, 1997–98 and 2013–15. Past returns do not guarantee future returns.
