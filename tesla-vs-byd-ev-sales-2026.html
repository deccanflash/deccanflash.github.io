Tesla vs BYD: who sells more electric cars?

1,629,957BYD battery-electric cars sold, Jan–Sep 2026 · Tesla: 1,324,681

BYD sold 305,276 more battery-electric cars than Tesla in the first nine months of 2026. In the July–September quarter BYD sold 762,478 against Tesla's 486,532, a lead of about 57%. Tesla was ahead only once in the last seven quarters, in January–March 2026.

Quick answers

Who sells more electric cars, Tesla or BYD?

BYD. It sold 1,629,957 battery-electric cars in January–September 2026, against Tesla's 1,324,681 deliveries. In 2025 BYD sold 2,256,714 to Tesla's 1,636,129, overtaking Tesla for a full year for the first time.

How many cars did Tesla deliver in Q3 2026?

486,532, down 2.1% from a year earlier but above market estimates. BYD sold 762,478 battery-electric cars in the same quarter, up 30.9%.

When did Tesla last beat BYD?

In January–March 2026, when Tesla delivered 358,023 cars against BYD's 310,389. BYD has been ahead in the two quarters since.
