Inflation: India vs the world

5.54%India's retail inflation, September 2026 · 4th of 10 major economies

India's prices are rising faster than in the US (3.7%), the Eurozone (3.8%) and China (0.9%), but far slower than in Argentina (33.4%) and Turkey (29.73%). At 5.54%, India is still below the RBI's 6% upper limit.

Quick answers

Is India's inflation higher than America's?

Yes. India's retail inflation was 5.54% in September 2026, against 3.7% in the United States in the same month.

Which country has the highest inflation?

Among these 10 major economies, Argentina, at 33.4%, followed by Turkey at 29.73% in September 2026.

Where does India rank?

4th of the 10 economies in this ranking, behind Argentina, Turkey and Russia (6.3%), and just ahead of Brazil (4.58%).

What is the RBI's inflation target?

4%, with a band of 2% to 6%. India's 5.54% is inside the band but above the 4% target.
