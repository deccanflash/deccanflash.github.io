Where are cars going electric?

25%of new cars sold in the world in 2025 were electric · India: 4%

In 2025, 1 in 4 new cars sold on Earth was electric. Norway has almost finished the switch at 97%, and in China more than half of new cars were electric. In India the figure is about 1 in 25.

Quick answers

Which country sells the most electric cars as a share of sales?

Norway. About 97% of new cars sold there in 2025 were electric, according to the IEA Global EV Outlook 2026.

What share of new cars sold in India are electric?

About 4% in 2025, or roughly 1 in 25 new cars, according to IEA data compiled by Pew Research Center.

What share of new cars worldwide are electric?

About 25% in 2025, or 1 in 4 new cars sold.

Are more than half of China's new cars electric?

Yes. About 53% of new cars sold in China in 2025 were battery-electric or plug-in hybrid.
