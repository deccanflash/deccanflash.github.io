UPI transactions in India

2,407 croreUPI transactions in September 2026 · up 23% on September 2025

Indians made about 80 crore UPI payments every day in September 2026. UPI handles roughly half of all real-time payments in the world.

Why UPI matters

  • It is free for people sending money and works across banks and apps.
  • Small shops accept it with a printed QR code, no card machine needed.
  • The global share figure (about 49%) comes from ACI Worldwide's 2024 real-time payments report.

Quick answers

How many UPI transactions happen in a month?

2,407 crore in September 2026, about 80 crore a day.

Who publishes UPI data?

NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India) publishes monthly figures early each month.
